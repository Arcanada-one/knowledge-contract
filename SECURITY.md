# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to security@arcanada.ai. Do not disclose a vulnerability through a public issue before coordinated disclosure.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours and complete triage within seven days. Our target remediation windows are 90 days for high or critical severity and 180 days for medium severity; low severity is handled on a best-effort basis. Coordinated public disclosure is targeted within 14 days after a fix or 120 days after the report, whichever is sooner.

The public key for security@arcanada.ai is available through keys.openpgp.org.
