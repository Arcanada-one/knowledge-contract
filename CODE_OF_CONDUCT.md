# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of someone else's private information. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [security@arcanada.ai](mailto:security@arcanada.ai). Include enough context to investigate, while avoiding unnecessary personal data. Maintainers will review reports, limit access to the information, and decide proportionate action. This contact is also listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
