# Project Support: Sponsoring Knowledge Contract

Knowledge Contract develops open knowledge and tools for connecting a knowledge artifact to its sources, dependencies, constraints, assessment and intended use. Sponsorship would help maintain this public work and make it easier to understand, check and reuse.

## What support would fund

- Maintaining the scientific texts and engineering specifications, with sources and explicit limits for each claim.
- Improving the public schemas, validation tools, examples and reproducible checks.
- Preparing accessible reader guides and reviewing translations.
- Maintaining the public reading site and improving navigation through the knowledge base.
- Reviewing contributions, correcting errors and publishing evidence for completed work.

The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) describes the work directions. The [studies and evidence](engineering/studies/README.md) show recorded results, reproduction procedures and their limits. A sponsorship proposal can identify a particular public work package, its deliverables and a reporting cadence.

## Open scope

Published text and documentation use [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Published code, schemas and tools use [Apache-2.0](LICENSE-CODE). Third-party materials retain their own rights and attribution; see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

This sponsorship scope covers the public knowledge, specifications and tools. The separate `contract-place-api` backend remains proprietary under the adopted [openness policy](OPENNESS.md). Sponsorship does not change that boundary or the licenses of published work.

Scientific claims continue to depend on their evidence. Sponsorship supports the work; published results and review decisions remain based on evidence and the project's contribution process.

## Sponsorship availability

A verified payment channel is not listed on this page yet. This page is preparation for sponsorship, not confirmation that a funding account is active or an application has been approved.

Once the maintainer has verified the receiving account and its approved public profile, that exact profile can be linked here and in the repository's Sponsor button. Private identity, banking and tax records belong in the funding provider's secure onboarding process.

For now, a prospective sponsor can open a public discussion through the [repository issues](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/issues), describing the public work they would like to support. A funding commitment and any specific deliverables need an explicit agreement with the maintainer.

## Contribute time and expertise

You can also review a paper, reproduce an engineering check, improve a tool, report an error, or help with a reader guide or translation. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for source attribution, review and licensing. Cite reused work through [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) and [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).
