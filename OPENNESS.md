# Openness and attribution policy

Status: adopted 2026-10-28 by operator order. This is the project's normative intent; the legal terms are the license files, not this page.

1. **Open project.** Knowledge Contract Arcana is public and open to community contribution and to free use, including commercial use and use as the base of your own project.
2. **Attribution is mandatory.** Reuse must credit the author and link to the source it was taken from ([ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md)). The project aims to collect a large share of what is known about meaning management, so the link back is the point of the exchange.
3. **Licenses.** Text, papers, specifications, site text, corpus and documentation: [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Code, schemas and tools: [Apache-2.0](LICENSE-CODE). No custom license: bespoke terms are not recognised by tooling and deter reuse. Derivative projects keep their own license (no share-alike).
4. **Sources stay credited.** Third-party works keep their own license. They are registered in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) and must stay credited in any derivative.
5. **Contribution.** Inbound equals outbound under the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): sign off every commit (`git commit -s`). There is no CLA. Contributors keep their copyright and are credited in the git history and, when substantial, in `CONTRIBUTORS` and `CITATION.cff`.
6. **Review before publication.** A paper or specification is published only after review; drafts and unpublished research packages are not published, and this policy applies to a paper when it is published. Review means a blind agent review under DEC-AUP-0010, recorded in a decision file and receipt (see [corpus/README.md](corpus/README.md)). It is run under the same account and model family as the author: the records are consistent with git but their authenticity is not checked, because the writer can fabricate them; the review checks the text, its sources and its license declarations and does not establish independence, identity of the author, copyright of agent-written text, or the absence of unrecognised third-party material. Publication does not establish scientific validity or authority to act.
7. **Names.** "Knowledge Contract Arcana", "Arcanada" and logos are not licensed. Saying "Based on Knowledge Contract Arcana" is fine; implying endorsement is not.
8. **Open knowledge, closed backend (decided).** The backend, `contract-place-api`, is the engine that processes, admits and evolves contracts. It stays closed and proprietary and its repository stays private; this is a decided boundary, not an open question. The knowledge, specifications, papers, schemas, site and reference code, as they are published, are open (CC BY 4.0 for text, Apache-2.0 for code, attribution required) and open to extension by the community and contributors.
