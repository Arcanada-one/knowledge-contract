# Contributing

Knowledge Contract research is in progress. Open an issue to discuss a proposed change before preparing a pull request. A proposal should state its scope, evidence, and the affected specification or document. Publication of a draft requires separate review; a submitted proposal does not imply adoption.

Keep contributions in English, provide sources for factual claims, and identify third-party material and its license. Do not include secrets, private data, or unpublished draft packages. Text contributions are offered under [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/LICENSE); code and schemas are offered under [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/LICENSE-CODE). Raise any licensing exception before submitting material. The project policy is in [OPENNESS.md](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/OPENNESS.md).

Report security concerns through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/SECURITY.md).

## Propose public content

Use the [pull request template](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/.github/pull_request_template.md) for each proposed paper, specification, guide, example, or correction. Identify the author or authorized rights holder, every source used for factual claims, the license of original and third-party material, the requested publication status, and the reviewer sought. Link to third-party text when reproduction rights are unclear; do not paste it into the repository.

A pull request is a proposal. Review must confirm both public publication rights and the intended status before a path can be added to the [corpus allowlist](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/corpus/README.md). Only a merged commit and an explicitly allowlisted path can become an indexing input. Publication does not establish scientific validity, current applicability, or agent authority to act.

For a correction, identify the earlier path and immutable revision, explain the changed claim, and request a `supersedes` relation. Keep the earlier revision traceable in Git history. For a withdrawal, state the affected path and revision privately if disclosure would cause harm; reviewers must remove it from the public allowlist and record the decision before another index generation is promoted.

## Sign off every commit (DCO)

Contributions are accepted under the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): add a `Signed-off-by:` line to every commit with `git commit -s`. There is no CLA. Inbound equals outbound: you license your contribution under the license of the file you change (CC BY 4.0 for text, Apache-2.0 for code and schemas), and you keep your copyright. Substantial contributors are credited in the git history and in `CONTRIBUTORS` and `CITATION.cff`. The sign-off must be in the last paragraph of the commit message and its name and email must match the commit's author or committer. CI fails a pull request that has a non-merge commit without such a sign-off (merge commits are not checked; the commits they bring are); automation commits from the allow-listed bot authors in `tools/check_dco.py` are exempt. Fix a missing sign-off with `git rebase --signoff` and a force-push of your own branch.

## Credit sources

Reuse of this project must credit the author and link to the source ([ATTRIBUTION.md](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/ATTRIBUTION.md)); contributions follow the same rule for what they build on. Register every third-party source of a paper or specification in [SOURCES.md](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/SOURCES.md), or declare `sources: none` in the file's front matter. CI fails a new file under `papers/` or `spec/` that has neither.

# Openness and attribution policy

8. **Open knowledge, closed backend (decided).** The backend, `contract-place-api`, is the engine that processes, admits and evolves contracts. It stays closed and proprietary and its repository stays private; this is a decided boundary, not an open question. The knowledge, specifications, papers, schemas, site and reference code, as they are published, are open (CC BY 4.0 for text, Apache-2.0 for code, attribution required) and open to extension by the community and contributors.

# Knowledge Contract Arcana

Knowledge Contract Arcana is part of the [Arcanada ecosystem](https://arcanada.ai/en/). It studies how people and agents can reuse knowledge while preserving source, scope, evidence, and conditions of use. This repository is a public scaffold for future rules, specifications, papers, and articles targeting Knowledge Contract `3.0.0-draft.1`; the research remains in progress, and no draft package is published here. Runtime adoption and empirical effectiveness are not claimed.

## Purpose

Knowledge Contract Arcana is about managing knowledge and its reuse by people and agents: contracts for managing the heritage of human knowledge. The contract is designed so that each piece of knowledge will travel with its source, author, scope and evidence.

It is designed to replace the hand-written skills, rules and prompt files that are kept separately in each agent harness with one unified knowledge contract. The contract is meant to evolve itself (changes admitted only on evidence, versioned, with rollback) and to improve through the community and contributors, so that it stays ahead of any single harness or skill set.

- **Working today:** the public hub at [contract.place](https://contract.place); the licensing and attribution mechanics (CC BY 4.0 for text, Apache-2.0 for code, [NOTICE](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/NOTICE), [ATTRIBUTION.md](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/ATTRIBUTION.md), [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/CITATION.cff), REUSE compliance, DCO sign-off, and a [source register](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/SOURCES.md) with a CI check); this repository as a public scaffold with rules, governance and tools; and two published articles on arcanada.ai (the architecture article and version 2).
- **Planned, not built:** published specifications and papers in this repository; a sourced corpus of human knowledge on meaning management (the [corpus allowlist](https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract/blob/39e05969728ea7a51f162cdbefac32fd8f54dbb6/corpus/README.md) has no approved entries yet); the unified contract that replaces per-harness skills, rules and prompt files; the self-evolution loop; community intake and review; knowledge selection with a provenance receipt for every answer; search and access for people and agents (graph and vector search, an API and an MCP server).
- **Open and closed:** whatever knowledge, specifications, papers, schemas and reference code are published here and on the site is open (see Licensing). The backend engine that processes, admits and evolves contracts stays closed and proprietary.

# Knowledge Contract Arcana

> Public home for Knowledge Contract research. The specification and experiments are in progress; 3.0.0-draft.1 is a target, not a released protocol.

- Home: https://contract.place/
- Knowledge Contract repository: https://github.com/Arcanada-one/knowledge-contract
- Site source: https://github.com/Arcanada-one/contract-place-site
- Ecosystem: https://arcanada.ai/
- Architecture article: https://arcanada.ai/en/blog/meaning-management-knowledge-contract-architecture
- Version 2 article: https://arcanada.ai/en/blog/meaning-management-knowledge-contract-v2

## Purpose

Knowledge Contract Arcana is about managing knowledge and its reuse by people and agents: contracts for managing the heritage of human knowledge. It is designed to replace hand-written skills, rules and prompt files kept separately in each agent harness with one unified knowledge contract that evolves itself (evidence-gated, versioned, with rollback) and improves through the community and contributors.

- Working today: public hub, licensing and attribution mechanics (CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0, NOTICE, ATTRIBUTION.md, CITATION.cff, REUSE, DCO, source register), the knowledge-contract repository as a public scaffold, and two published articles on arcanada.ai.
- Planned, not built: published specifications and papers in the repository, a sourced corpus (no approved entries yet), the unified contract for harnesses, the self-evolution loop, community intake and review, knowledge selection with provenance receipts, search and access (graph and vector search, an API and an MCP server).
- The backend engine that processes, admits and evolves contracts stays closed and proprietary; whatever knowledge is published is open.

No API, graph, vector, or MCP service is offered on this site yet.

## Rule

Every file added under `papers/` or `spec/` must be covered in one of two ways:

1. A row in the table below whose **Where** column contains the file path in backticks, or
2. A `sources:` key in the file's front matter (Markdown only): either a non-empty list in which every entry equals the **Source** cell of a row below, or the literal `sources: none` when the file uses no external source. `sources: []`, `sources: ""` and an empty key do not count.
