## Abstract

The paper proposes an operational research programme for managing transitions between a source, a representation of its content, a bounded context and the result of applying it. Its main question is when a person or an agent can reuse previously expressed content while keeping essential differences, grounds and limits under a finite budget of attention, memory and computation. The main text offers context-sufficiency boundaries, conditional properties of composition and a refutation programme. Cooking, navigation and circuit design serve synthetic analyses and limited three-book observations, not transfer to other texts. Executed appendix runs are limited and do not establish extraction quality.

## Claims and their basis

- Conditional results (the F-series) hold only under their stated premises; a previously checked artifact keeps its protected properties only if the dependency manifest is complete. Basis type: proof-under-premises. No measured reuse result is claimed.
- A synthetic example with three necessary parts costing 2, 2 and 3 against a budget of 5 totals 7, so the obligation cannot be met in one package. Basis type: proof-under-premises; an arithmetic example, not a measurement.
- "Minimal context" may be claimed only with a declared option space, cost function and optimality proof. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- Six programme hypotheses each name a strong comparison and a refuting outcome. Basis type: hypothesis; none is tested, and no row-level status is asserted.
- Appendix experiment on pre-structured material, 80 calls. Rubric A met 193 and left 7 unknown of 200 criteria; rubric B met 197 and left 3 unknown of 200. Vectors matched in 13 of 20 canonical pairs and differed in 7. Basis type: finite-test; five of five calibration controls apply to one profile only. The source MM_A5295 was NOT read by the card writer; claims resting on it keep the limited wording above.
- Release cross-check by the kit authors: 13 of 14 first answers passed the narrow conditions, and in one task (T09) the first output was rejected because the register of a protected phrase changed. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; a self-check by one assistant, not independent, and the 14 editing tasks do not test this model.

## Limits

- The 12 refuting probes were editorial constructions and were not run at the initial stage; their later status is not claimed.
- OCR, formulas, tables, diagrams and the 917 derived descriptions behind the three-book observations were not independently verified.
- Three domains yield different defect types; they do not show transfer to other texts or professions.
- Universality of the general model is not proved.

## Abstract

Reusing knowledge requires transformations: normalisation, reduction, joining sources, inference, parametrisation and transfer to a new situation. The paper proposes a transition contract linking input editions, the permitted effect, preconditions, postconditions, protected properties, assumptions, losses and the actual output. Geometric similarity only selects candidates. - Ten conditional properties CP01-CP10 are arguments under explicit premises, not proofs and not empirical results. A worked three-step chain shows that a dropped condition is not restored by an old report. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.

## Abstract

The paper treats reuse of prepared results of extraction, retrieval, composition and professional preparation as a separate admission operation. Reusing a prepared result requires a match with the new request, current constraints, required grounds and the recipient's profile. The paper formalises this boundary and states conditional properties, an economic formulation, strong alternatives and grounds for refutation. The outcome is a research model and a finite executable profile. Effectiveness on real books, causal usefulness and industrial correctness remain hypotheses. The one measured efficiency result, a local paired pilot, found no stable gain.

## Claims and their basis

- The central question has two halves: admissibility of carrying a result between requests, which admits conditional proofs, and efficiency at full cost, which stays a hypothesis. Basis type: hypothesis.
- Main conditional property: if the checked properties are finite, the dependency set contains every input affecting them, and the transformation preserves them, they hold after reuse. It is a conditional lemma with a justification and a finite model check, not a mechanised proof; the justification was not read (NOT VERIFIED), and completeness for real content is not claimed. Basis type: proof-under-premises.
- Local exact-versus-off pilot: three pairs, 144 measured requests each, 432 exact-mode reads; latency ratios 1.0185, 0.9982, 0.8017; median 0.9982. No stable gain; no semantic compiler or model, so no token or compilation saving. Basis type: finite-test.
- In R42 all 60 executions matched expected outcomes and no listed violation occurred, yet 36 assessments kept an unresolved mapping and 12 an unknown new condition. The simple branch equalled the graph branch in all 30 cases, with a shared validator. A synthetic cost example gives 10000 versus 1740 units with break-even at N=18; a second example gives a loss of 10849; the executable example (MM_A0453) was not read and 10849 could not be reproduced from the paper's parameters. Basis type: finite-test.
- The contribution is a problem formulation; novelty of the integration is not established. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- A later cross-check by the kit authors, self-checked by one assistant and not independent, reports 13 of 14 first text results passing all conditions and 14 of 14 technical replays against knowledge contracts. Context economy is not measured. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.

## Limits

- Not established: completeness of dependency manifests for natural language, real error rates, causal usefulness.
- The pilot is one local runtime and did not compare a compilation level with an exact-match cache.
- Untested: harm to rare cases, utility-assessment cost above the gain, false completeness after insertion on real content.

## Abstract

The paper proposes a way to link the life cycle of meaning artifacts to observed resource use, conditions of application and results for specific recipients. The object of evaluation is not a universal "value of a text" but the contribution of an artifact version to a goal fixed in advance, relative to an explicit alternative. Costs, including those of the evaluation itself, are counted. The paper offers a synthesis and a contract profile, not a value theory. No retention or ranking policy based on conditional utility has been tested on a real load; the appendix holds only local resource observations from runs R38-R44.

## Claims and their basis

- Four levels are separated, from cost incurred to estimated added contribution. A scalar utility is admissible only for a concrete decision with a published conversion policy; no comparison with a vector was run. Causal usefulness was not estimated (NOT VERIFIED against MM_A0428 or the causal literature, not read). Complaint scenarios A to E are constructed and none was executed. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- Central claim: a utility-managed policy improves the cost of a correct result against strong simple alternatives in a given load. No load was studied and no comparison was made. Basis type: hypothesis.
- Graph and simple implementations agreed in 30 of 30 targeted pairs (R42), with no economic advantage. In R39 the raw `met` sums (193 and 197) count criteria, not fully correct results, and were an unusable denominator. Basis type: finite-test.
- R43 had 8 pairs and 16 answers, and R44 had 5 pairs and 24 projections, with equal results and no economic advantage; neither tests the utility policy. Basis type: hypothesis.
- A later cross-check by the kit authors, self-checked by one assistant and not independent, separates a subject self-check on 14 cases from efficiency. The 14 cases are 15 text versions, not 15 trials. No measured gain, cost, tokens or latency and no matched baseline or blind reviewer are recorded. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.

## Limits

- No price of knowledge, causal usefulness or right to payment is established.
- R42 timing includes a shared validator and is not product latency.
- In R43, 7 of 153 non-empty remarks went to limited checking and 146 did not; this is a routing policy, not a proven saving of 146 OCR calls.
- A cheaper flat implementation may exist; this is open.
