## Abstract

Reusing knowledge requires transformations: normalisation, reduction, joining sources, inference, parametrisation and transfer to a new situation. Such operations can give outwardly identical results while preserving different properties and resting on different grounds. The paper proposes a transition contract linking input editions, the permitted effect, preconditions, postconditions, protected properties, assumptions, losses and the actual output. Geometric similarity only selects candidates. Conditional properties are arguments under explicit premises, and hypotheses about the benefit of the profile are untested; the appendix keeps limited executed experiments that do not confirm them. It does not claim a universal computable algebra of human meaning.

## Claims and their basis

- The architecture has three levels (content, operation, execution); substituting one for another produces false promises, since a correct schema does not guarantee correct inference. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; a design stance, not a theorem.
- Ten conditional properties CP01-CP10 are arguments under explicit premises, not proofs and not empirical results. A worked three-step chain shows that a dropped condition is not restored by an old report. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- Four hypotheses need empirical evaluation. Basis type: hypothesis; the appendix does not establish them.
- Appendix logs for the three books hold 100 culinary, 41 automatic and 28 navigation operations, all marked `auto_applied_unreviewed`. The first literal mapping linked 100 of 100, 41 of 41 and only 21 of 28; a later mapping reproduced and reversed all 28, and the failed first attempt is kept. Basis type: finite-test.
- Vectors matched in 13 of 20 canonical pairs, which is not evidence that the carriers are equivalent; 7 of 20 differed. Basis type: finite-test.
- A release cross-check by the kit authors tested idempotence and join for skill-dependency closure on all 4096 subsets of 12 skills. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; a self-check by one assistant, covering finite-traversal laws, not an algebra of meaning, with geometry not implemented.

## Limits

- The navigation readable file did not reproduce whole, and three captions fell into alt-text; reversing an operation over one block is not restoring the full artifact.
- The finite profile does not test recognition of arbitrary phrases, implicit domain models or visual understanding. No execution report of it was read.
- Practical confirmation needs permitted pages of three books, fixed OCR/VLM versions and independent labelling; counts of documents are not a measure of truth.
- The separation draws on prior work and is not presented as a new mathematical result.
