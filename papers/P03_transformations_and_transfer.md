## Abstract

Reusing knowledge requires transformations: normalisation, reduction, joining sources, inference, parametrisation and transfer to a new situation. Such operations can give outwardly identical results while preserving different properties and resting on different grounds. The paper proposes a transition contract linking input editions, the permitted effect, preconditions, postconditions, protected properties, assumptions, losses and the actual output. It treats composition of partial transformations, equivalence classes, directed entailment, task projection and reconstruction. It does not claim a universal computable algebra of human meaning; geometric similarity is only a way to select candidates.

## Claims and their basis

- The architecture has three levels (content, operation, execution), and substituting one for another produces false promises: a correct schema does not guarantee correct inference. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment; a design stance, not a theorem.
- Ten conditional properties CP01-CP10 are stated as arguments under explicit premises, not empirical results. A worked three-step chain shows that a condition dropped at one step is not restored by a technically available old report. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- Four hypotheses (separating equivalence, projection, inference and acceptance; protected properties and maps; a common part beating plain duplicate removal; pinned rules and dependencies) need empirical evaluation. Basis type: hypothesis; the appendix experiments do not establish them.
- Appendix operation logs for the three books hold 100 culinary, 41 automatic and 28 navigation operations, all marked `auto_applied_unreviewed`. The first literal mapping of Markdown offsets linked 100 of 100, 41 of 41 and only 21 of 28; a revised mapping version later reproduced and reversed all 28, with the failed first attempt kept. Basis type: finite-test.
- Vectors matched in 13 of 20 canonical pairs, which is not evidence that the carriers are equivalent; 7 of 20 differed. Basis type: finite-test.

## Limits

- The navigation readable file did not reproduce whole, and three captions fell into alt-text; reversing an operation over one block is not restoring the full artifact.
- A finite profile (exact rational arithmetic, truth tables, partial transition tables, bounded coordinate transforms, pinned references) does not test recognition of arbitrary phrases, implicit domain models or visual understanding. No execution report was available to this summary.
- Practical confirmation needs permitted pages of three books, fixed OCR/VLM versions, independent labelling and implemented adapters. Counts of documents or links are not a measure of truth.
