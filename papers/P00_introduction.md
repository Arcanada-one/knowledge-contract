## Abstract

The paper proposes an operational research programme for managing transitions between a source, a representation of its content, a bounded context and the result of applying it. Its main question is under which conditions a person or an agent can reuse previously expressed content while keeping essential differences, grounds and limits, given a finite budget of attention, memory and computation. The unit of analysis is a checkable transition, not a text fragment. The paper offers context-sufficiency boundaries, conditional properties of composition and a refutation programme, illustrated in cooking, navigation and circuit design. It does not claim a theory of human meaning or a catalogue of all knowledge.

## Claims and their basis

- Conditional results (the F-series) hold only under their stated model premises; for example, a previously checked artifact keeps its protected properties only if the dependency manifest is complete and current rights allow issue. Basis type: proof-under-premises. No measured reuse result is claimed.
- A synthetic budget example (parts costing 2, 2 and 3 against a budget of 5) shows that an obligation can be infeasible in one package. Basis type: proof-under-premises; arithmetic example, not a measurement.
- "Minimal context" may be claimed only with a declared option space, cost function and optimality proof. Basis type: authors-domain-assessment.
- Six programme hypotheses (explicit conditions, addressable dependencies, separate coverage, budgeted assembly, reuse cost, shared artifacts) each name a strong comparison and a refuting outcome. Basis type: hypothesis; all six are untested.
- An appendix experiment on pre-structured material: 80 calls; rubric A met 193 and left 7 unknown of 200 criteria, rubric B met 197 and left 3 unknown of 200; 20 of 20 change probes met; vectors matched in 13 of 20 pairs and differed in 7. Basis type: finite-test. Five of five calibration controls apply to one profile only.

## Limits

- The 12 refuting probes were editorial constructions and were not run at the initial stage; their later status is not claimed.
- The three-book observations are limited; OCR, formulas, tables, diagrams and the 917 derived descriptions were not independently verified.
- Three domains yield different defect types; they do not show transfer to arbitrary texts or professions.
- Novelty is not claimed for any single entity, graph, contract or cache. The result may reduce to an engineering composition of known means.
