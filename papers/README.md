The source catalogue at `6a6f202f7f70be2ad3aea3369e45409761708596` includes two research-draft foundations (Meaning Bridge and Meaning Geometry) alongside the eight established edition-1.1 papers. Start with [Meaning Bridge](human-meaning-and-computable-knowledge/PAPER.md), then follow the [typed corpus map](../reference/foundations/README.md). Both foundations retain their research-draft status: a source reading entry is not independent acceptance, a new released edition or corpus-index admission.

The current catalogue contains eleven papers, including the new [Agent Situation](agent-situation/PAPER.md) research draft.

The existing papers develop the formal and methodological argument. Each states its assumptions, conditional results, counterexamples, limits, and the engineering evidence relevant to its claims. An edition is not a claim of external peer review or universal empirical effectiveness. The [correction record](../reviews/edition-corrections/REVIEW.md) identifies the prior review findings and their disposition.
