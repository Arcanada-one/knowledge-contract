# Meaning Management papers

Fourteen papers of the Meaning Management research programme (P00 to P13), the first release in this repository. Each paper has an English entry page (a short summary with its claims, their basis and its limits) and a Russian full text. All of it is a scientific draft: concept-level results with limited finite checks, not an adopted standard, a measured product or a proof of usefulness.

## Language and status

- The full texts are Russian originals. The English entry pages are summaries, not translations, and do not replace the originals. A full English translation has not been started.
- Every paper keeps its permanent artifact id; the revision id of the text published here is stated in its front matter and in the release catalog.
- The late research synthesis gate is open. Independent semantic review of the papers has not been performed. The editorial notes behind the English entry pages check wording against the cited basis; they do not check scientific truth.
- The Russian texts are the terminology edition of 2026-11-03. Relative to that edition the release changed only front-matter keys (artifact id, revision id, licence), one assembly comment and link targets, as recorded in [the release catalog](../receipts/meaning-management/release-catalog.json) and its [link map](../receipts/meaning-management/link-rewrites.json). Exact edition digests are in the catalog.

## Markers in the Russian text

The papers cite many supporting records that are not part of this release. A link to a record that is released is rewritten to its repository path. A link to a record that is not released is shown as its text followed by a marker: `(MM_A..., not in this release)` names the supporting record by artifact id, `(restricted external basis)` marks material derived from third-party books that is not republished, and `(glossary, not in this release)` marks the terminology glossary. Such a marker is not an active link and promises no later access.
