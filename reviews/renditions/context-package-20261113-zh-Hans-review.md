## Full-text findings beyond the numbered clauses

**Purpose and conformance:** The rendition preserves the architectural status, need for a named representation profile, limited implemented exchange subset, distinct book-package schema, and absence of an implied full wire format or deployed runtime. It preserves the versioned, budgeted and purpose-bound package definition. A supporting package is still neither an execution grant nor evidence that the result occurred.

**Selection and closure:** Active mandatory relations are distinguished from navigation links. Unknown or missing endpoints prevent a complete-package claim. Cycles do not invalidate finite abstract closure. These qualifications are present in addition to the numbered requirements.

**Coverage and assurance:** All five coverage tokens remain literal: `supported`, `partial`, `unsupported`, `conflicted`, `unknown`. Their definitions preserve established support, named missing obligations, procedure-relative unsupported status, incompatible grounds and insufficient observations. In particular, unknown does not become unsupported or false. The three literal search-assurance tokens are unchanged and in source order: `candidate_only`, `exhaustive_in_scope`, `incomplete_procedure`. Two examples do not require exhaustive search; all matching objects impose an additional completeness obligation. Candidate support does not certify a truncated package, and package support does not certify proper output use.

**Budget and projection:** Character count remains distinct from exact token measurement. Exact reconstruction of selected bytes still does not establish semantic completeness when selection omitted an exception. Byte equality and task sufficiency retain different checks.

**Delivery:** The manifest states intended delivery; the receipt records observed delivery at the recipient boundary. A media URL establishes none of byte availability, display, legibility or understanding. Receipt/comprehension acknowledgement remains distinct from capability assessment, execution agreement and authorization.

**Evidence and compatibility:** The study paragraph preserves **4,096 subsets of 12 skill roots**, **24,576 single-element inclusion relations** and a **110-node graph**. It does not turn the finite graph result into complete extraction from arbitrary sources. Projection minimality remains bounded by the reported measurement. Legacy coverage imports retain their subject and semantics; missing observations remain unknown; a new edition does not retrospectively turn examples into executed packages. Historical experimental claims were compared for fidelity, not independently rerun or authenticated.
