### P1 — Material: receipt-time rebinding can certify an obsolete derivation

`Model.ingest` assigns `dependency_versions` from the model's current versions when a derived observation arrives. That is not necessarily the version used when the derivation was observed or produced.

The following valid API sequence was executed with distinct source streams and TTL 20:

1. Observe `a = old` at time 0, receipt 0, sequence 1.
2. Observe `a = new` at time 5, receipt 5, sequence 2.
3. Receive `b = derived from OLD a`, observation time 0, receipt 10, dependency `a`.
4. Resolve and project required `b` at time 10.

**Observed:** `a = new`, `b.state = KNOWN`, `projection.status = READY`, and `b` was bound to dependency version 2. An old derived observation was thereby attached to newer premises at receipt. This extends the predeclared control 8 beyond its favorable arrival ordering and concerns controls 3 and 15 as well.

**Suggested repair:** accept explicit, checked derivation-time dependency pins, or conservatively return NOT_READY/NOT_IMPLEMENTED when the binding is unproven. Blocking a dependency observed after the derivation is one useful counterexample check, but timestamp comparison alone does not prove historical dependency identity. Add a delayed-derivation adverse case and a valid same-binding positive case. Preserve the original evidence rather than rewriting it as if the case had always passed.

### P2 — Secondary: missing snapshot version coverage passes validation

After creating a ready snapshot, clearing its `versions` mapping, and changing a critical prerequisite, `validate` returned `VALID`. The validator iterates supplied versions but does not verify that they cover the recorded closure.

The README excludes adversarial snapshot authentication, so this is not a claim of a security boundary violation. Nevertheless, accidental mutation or incomplete deserialization can bypass the stated critical-change check.

**Suggested repair:** verify snapshot shape, exact version-key/closure coverage, nonnegative integer versions, identity and required closure before comparing current values. Return INVALID for malformed snapshots. This improves structural integrity without claiming cryptographic authenticity.

### M1 — Required paper correction: revision equality requires change coverage

Section 7 currently concludes that a changed prerequisite cannot pass an equality-based revision check. The assumptions only require relevant source revisions and an authoritative current source. An authoritative source can still change a value while retaining or recycling its revision token.

**Suggested replacement:** “If an authoritative current scoped revision differs from the recorded revision, exact comparison detects that difference. Detecting every material prerequisite change additionally assumes that each such change produces a distinct, non-reused token, bound to the same source, resource, account and prerequisite identity.” Retain the existing check/effect race limitation. This property concerns declared revision semantics, not a proof that the source reports every change.

Observation-age preservation is correct under the stated clock/timestamp assumptions. Mandatory-closure preservation is correct relative to the declared graph and projector behavior; its explicit incompleteness and interpretation caveats are appropriate. Conflict retention is a conservative representation policy, not conflict resolution.

### M2 — Required consistency correction: full history is an unequal-budget reference

Section 8 says all baselines share budgets. The proposed experiment intentionally gives full history up to 16,384 evidence tokens and the matched conditions 4,096.

**Fix:** state that the matched-budget comparisons share evidence budgets; identify full history as a separately costed unequal-budget reference. Continue counting maintenance and indexing costs.

### M3 — Clarify revocation deduplication

Section 5.2's warning that stop or revocation events cannot be discarded as ordinary duplicates is overbroad. An exact logical duplicate of a successfully applied revocation can safely be suppressed under the prototype's idempotency rule.

**Fix:** prohibit coalescing that loses effective revocation/stop state or required causal information; distinguish that from suppression of a verified logical duplicate. Do not imply all repeated revocation messages require renewed application.

### M4 — Clarify additive budget accounting

Section 6's sum is suitable as a declared abstract accounting model or a conservative bound. It is not automatically an exact token count: serialization, shared framing, tool schemas and token boundaries matter.

**Fix:** distinguish planning costs from a final fit check over the complete serialized request with the target model's accounting. Preserve the existing statement that prototype units are abstract and fit does not prove comprehension.

## Prior-art and scope assessment

LongMemEval-V2, Supersede, ReAct, Generative Agents, MemGPT and Lost in the Middle are represented within their inspected scope. The paper does not transfer historical benchmark rates into current deployment claims. EnvTrustBench and Scroll metadata/abstracts were rechecked at their pinned arXiv versions; the paper's limited descriptions are supported. Scroll remains abstract-only in this review.

**Closest-prior-art warning:** EnvTrustBench already discusses provenance, freshness, verification and evidence-guided action; Scroll describes a typed persistent session environment and printed model-facing projections. Consequently “live action decisions” or “typed context outside the prompt” alone is not an established novelty distinction. The paper's narrow claim of a particular KC integration and proposed evaluation is preferable. This targeted review cannot establish publication priority.

Initialization is appropriately profile-dependent and bounded. The separation between observations, interpreted state, model projection, intention and authority is coherent. The paper does not silently define a new normative protocol. Robotic and situation-awareness references were checked for consistency with the literature matrix, but their full methods were not independently re-audited in this pass.
