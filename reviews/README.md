# Publication Reviews

The [edition corrections record](edition-corrections/REVIEW.md) is the current response to the independent review supplied on 4 November 2026. It identifies the reviewed source revision, every finding, the changed documents, executed checks and remaining limits.

The [edition 1.0 integration review](publication-integrity/REVIEW.md) and its recorded input hashes are historical records. The later review found two invalid scientific inferences and several engineering defects that the earlier internal reviewers missed. Their acceptance therefore does not establish correctness of those claims, and it does not cover the changed bytes of edition 1.1. The original scientific and evidence review reports remain unchanged for accountability.

Review findings, mechanical conformance, publication approval and runtime admission are separate observations. An internal agent review is not external peer review, and a correction does not amend a historical receipt or admit new content into the search index.
