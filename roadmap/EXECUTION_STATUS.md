## Boundaries that remain

Availability is not a global acceptance verdict or unlimited resource budget. Current authorization, source and rendition admission, independent review, the actual CI result, merge, deployment, served readback and index admission retain their separate meanings.

No new costly experiment, credentials/access expansion, or unrelated protected operation follows merely from restored capacity. Use the applicable explicit mandate and the established process. A candidate document, green structure test or mailbox delivery does not discharge those conditions.

A new resource failure must identify its actual operation, time, environment, evidence, and effect on the next step. Continue independent useful work and use an available permitted execution route. Do not turn a dated single-worker limit into a standing ban on all engineering.
